# Video demo assets

The landscape and portrait MP4 files in `client/public/veylo/video/` are original Veylo motion graphics. They were generated by `client/scripts/create-video-demo.mjs` from mathematical colour fields and a generated audio tone. They contain no third-party footage, photographs, music, faces or client information.

The demo calls them animations and labels the studio as a demonstration. They demonstrate real MP4 playback, aspect ratios, seeking and original audio. They do not prove private Stream delivery, provider analytics, long-film credential renewal, or performance on a physical phone. Those remain separate release checks in the deployment guide.

Regenerate with `node client/scripts/create-video-demo.mjs`. Set `FFMPEG_PATH` on a platform without the repository's Windows Remotion FFmpeg binary.
